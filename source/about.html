Our Journey

From Detailing to Government Contracts

Car Wash Services didn't start in a boardroom. It started by hand — detailing cars, building a reputation on quality work, and earning business one customer at a time.

As demand grew, our founder hired help and began taking on larger commercial jobs. During slower seasons, opportunities arose to purchase equipment and client lists from companies that had gone out of business. One of those opportunities led to work with the United States Marine Corps, providing car detailing services on a base in San Diego — and that changed everything.

While working on-site, our founder helped repair a struggling car wash facility for Marine Corps Community Services (MCCS). What started as a simple repair quickly turned into ongoing maintenance work — and an unexpected introduction to government contracting. After studying Federal Acquisition Regulations (FAR) and developing compliant proposals, Car Wash Management successfully secured contracts to service and maintain car wash operations on military installations.

That success quickly expanded. Marine Corps Base Hawaii requested the same services, leading to the formation of Car Wash Management as a dedicated company focused on scaling operations, improving efficiency, and delivering long-term performance. From there, the business grew into warehousing, distribution, and ongoing service partnerships — laying the foundation for what we do today.

Where We Are Today

Focused on results. Not words on the wall.

Today, Car Wash Services works with both government installations and private operators — providing chemistry programs, preventive maintenance plans, equipment selection, and operational consulting under one roof.

We've been inside the bays, fixed the pumps, dialed the chemistry, and trained the staff. That firsthand experience is why our recommendations hold up in the real world — not just on paper.
