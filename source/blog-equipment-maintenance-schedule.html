Car Wash Equipment Maintenance Schedule — Complete Checklist for 2026

Complete car wash equipment maintenance schedule with daily, weekly, and monthly checklists. Prevent downtime and extend equipment life with our expert guide.

Back to Blog • Published:

Preventive maintenance reduces equipment failures by 83% and extends equipment life by 3-5 years. This comprehensive schedule covers daily, weekly, monthly, and quarterly tasks for all major car wash systems.

Daily Maintenance Tasks

  • Check water levels in all tanks
  • Inspect spray nozzles for clogs
  • Test emergency stop buttons
  • Verify chemical levels and refill
  • Clean vacuum filters
  • Inspect conveyor belt tension
  • Check tire shine applicator levels
  • Test payment systems and card readers

Weekly Maintenance Tasks

  • Lubricate conveyor chains and bearings
  • Clean reclaim tank sediment
  • Inspect high-pressure pump seals
  • Check belt alignment and wear
  • Test all safety sensors
  • Clean photoeye sensors
  • Inspect electrical panels for corrosion
  • Flush chemical injection lines

Monthly Maintenance Tasks

  • Replace vacuum filters
  • Inspect motor brushes and commutators
  • Check hydraulic fluid levels
  • Test GFCI outlets
  • Calibrate chemical dispensers
  • Inspect all hoses for wear/cracking
  • Clean electrical contactors
  • Check bearing temperatures with IR thermometer
