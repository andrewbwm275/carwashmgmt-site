Our Story

Founded on Grit

Car Wash Management didn't start in a boardroom—it started with determination and a willingness to do whatever it took.

After losing a job, our founder began offering mobile car detailing services out of the back of an old Honda Civic. With limited equipment and a lot of persistence, he went door to door in local neighborhoods and businesses, washing, waxing, and detailing cars. As demand grew, he hired help and began taking on larger commercial jobs.

During slower seasons, opportunities arose to purchase equipment and client lists from companies that had gone out of business. One of those opportunities led to work with the United States Marine Corps, providing car detailing services on a base in San Diego.

OUR JOURNEY CONTINUES

From Detailing to Government Contracts

While working on-site, our founder helped repair a struggling car wash facility for Marine Corps Community Services (MCCS). What started as a simple repair quickly turned into ongoing maintenance work—and an unexpected introduction to government contracting.

After studying Federal Acquisition Regulations (FAR) and developing compliant proposals, Car Wash Management successfully secured contracts to service and maintain car wash operations on military installations.

That success quickly expanded. Marine Corps Base Hawaii requested the same services, leading to the formation of Car Wash Management as a dedicated company focused on scaling operations, improving efficiency, and delivering long-term performance.

From there, the business grew into warehousing, distribution, and ongoing service partnerships—laying the foundation for what we do today.
