Not a boardroom origin.

Detailing from a Civic. Then Marine Corps Community Services. Then chemistry and PM.

Origin

Civic, then MCCS, then a plant.

After losing a job, the founder offered mobile detailing from an old Honda Civic, going door to door.

Slow seasons meant buying equipment and client lists from shops that had closed. One of those paths led to the United States Marine Corps.

On-site he helped repair a struggling MCCS car wash. Repair became maintenance. Studying FAR and writing compliant proposals led to contracts on military installations, including Marine Corps Base Hawaii, plus warehousing and distribution.

Integrity. Transparency. Compassion. No awards theater. No invented quotes.
