Privacy Policy

Car Wash Services LLC, doing business as Car Wash Management — Last modified: August 15, 2026

Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Car Wash Services LLC, doing business as Car Wash Management ("Car Wash Management," "we," "us," or "our"), collects, uses, and shares information from users of www.carwashmgmt.com and people who communicate with us by SMS, email, phone, or in person.

Information We Collect

We collect contact details you provide (name, email, mobile phone number, company, city/state), quote and calculator inputs, employment-application details you submit, and messages you send to us. When you opt in to SMS, we also store your mobile number, the time and method of consent, and your opt-out status.

How We Use It

We use this information to respond to inquiries, fulfill product or service requests, provide customer-care updates, follow up on appointments and applications, and improve our website. We do not sell your personal information.

SMS / Mobile Messaging

The only opt-in method for this program is texting START to +1 619-914-6819. We do not collect SMS consent through a website form, checkbox, paper form, or in-person process. Consent is not a condition of purchase.

After you text START, Car Wash Management may send two-way customer-care text messages about orders, deliveries, appointments, applications/onboarding, and replies to questions you send us. Message frequency varies. You may receive up to 8 customer-care messages per month, plus replies to messages you send us. Message and data rates may apply.

Reply STOP to opt out. Reply HELP for help, email info@carwashmgmt.com, or call 808-465-2291. Reply START to resubscribe after opting out.

We do not share, sell, or provide your mobile phone number or messaging consent data to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. We use Twilio only as our SMS delivery processor to send messages you consented to receive. Information obtained as part of the SMS consent process is not shared with third parties or affiliates.

A2P copies (do not replace): https://legal.carwashmgmt.com/privacy-policy.html

Cookies and analytics

Our website may use cookies and analytics tools to understand site usage and keep the site working. This website analytics data is separate from SMS consent. Mobile numbers collected for SMS are not used to build third-party advertising audiences.

Service providers

We may use vendors that host this website, process email, or (for SMS) transmit texts on our behalf. Those processors may handle data only to provide the service we requested. They are not given mobile numbers or SMS consent data to market their own products or to sell onward.

Legal disclosures

We may disclose information if required by law, to protect rights and safety, or in connection with a merger or sale of the business, in which case the successor will honor this policy for SMS consent data.

Your rights

You may request access, correction, or deletion of personal information by emailing info@carwashmgmt.com. You can stop SMS at any time by texting STOP.
